AFTERLIGHT // SHOP

SHOP

Spend generator cash to unlock new survivor roles.

CASH: $0

DOCTOR ROLE

Heal a CPU survivor in an adjacent space directly beside you (not diagonally). Press Q to restore up to 30 HP. Cooldown: 10 seconds.

SWORD ROLE

Unlock the sword-wielding survivor. Get close to the killer and press Q to stun them briefly. Each stun earns $5. Cooldown: 18 seconds.

BLOCK ROLE

Fight bare-handed. Press Q to block for 2 seconds (10-second cooldown). Blocking an attack unlocks one punch on E; each punch stun earns $5. Punches have no cooldown.

TRAPPER ROLE

Place one trap at your position with Q. The CPU killer is stunned for 3 seconds when stepping on it. After it triggers, wait 10 seconds before placing another.
